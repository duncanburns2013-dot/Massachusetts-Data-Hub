This moved into the Immigration dashboard

Massachusetts shelter and immigrant services spending now lives on the Money Trail section of the Immigration dashboard, alongside the rest of the immigration data rather than beside it on a page of its own.

The move folded two overlapping treatments of the same question into one. The dashboard's old vendor-name tabs — which caught three community health centres and a dentist on a substring match — are gone, replaced by the appropriation-based classification that was built to supersede them.

  • What it cost — enacted against actual, by fiscal year
  • Who got paid — every organisation, checked against the IRS exempt file
  • What isn't disclosed — the boundary, stated rather than modelled
  • If it were all public — the estimator, with its inputs exposed
  • Method — how the accounts were chosen, and what is deliberately left out
