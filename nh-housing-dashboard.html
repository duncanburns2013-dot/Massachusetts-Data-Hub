Salem is the hottest market in southern NH with a 101.67% sale-to-list ratio and a median of just 7 days on market. Derry follows at 100.71% SP/LP with 8 median DOM. Portsmouth commands nearly double the statewide median at $890K but moves slower (35 avg DOM) and has a lower sell-through rate (78.35%), typical of luxury markets. Windham has crossed the $1M average sale price threshold — a milestone for this I-93 corridor suburb.
