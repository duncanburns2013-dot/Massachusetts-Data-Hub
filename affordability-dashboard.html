What It Costs to Live in Massachusetts

Massachusetts is not uniformly expensive. This is what actually costs more, where in the state it costs more, and what a household has to earn to cover it.

⚖
The headline claim, tested. A high price level only bites if incomes do not keep up with it. The BEA publishes both halves — a price level for every state (Regional Price Parities, US = 100) and per-capita personal income deflated by that same price level. Comparing them is the cleanest available test of whether Massachusetts residents are, on average, behind.
