Labor Market Overview
Massachusetts lost 7,200 jobs in February — breaking a five-month streak. Unemployment ticked up to 4.8%

🔻 Job Growth Streak Broken

−7,200 jobs in February 2026 — Massachusetts lost payroll jobs for the first time since September 2025, breaking a five-month growth streak. Declines led by Ed/Health (−3,400), Trade/Transport (−2,100), and Leisure/Hospitality (−1,800). Source: BLS CES, USDL-26-0651.

⚠️ Unemployment Ticks Back Up

MA unemployment at 4.8% in February 2026 — up from revised 4.7% in January, and still +0.4 pts above national rate of 4.4%. Note: December 2025 was revised DOWN to 4.7% from 4.8%.
