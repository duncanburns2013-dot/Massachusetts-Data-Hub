✅ March 2026 — Rebound

+6,800 jobs in March 2026, rebounding from −7,100 in February. Six-month total Oct 2025–Mar 2026: +12,700 jobs. Gains led by healthcare and services. Source: BLS CES via Mass.gov EOLWD, May 1, 2026.

NFP Benchmark Revision — National Payroll Overcount

📊

Context matters: The 6-month gain of +12,700 comes after MA added only 1,300 jobs in all of 2025 — the weakest year since the pandemic. Payrolls are recovering but the household survey tells a different story: employed persons fell ~42,000 since September, as residents drop out of the labor force due to retirements and declining immigration.

📊

What the loss of monthly state JOLTS means: Job openings, quit rates, and hire rates were the best real-time indicators of MA labor demand. Without them, the only monthly MA-specific data is unemployment (LAUS) and payrolls by sector (CES). The annual release in July 2026 will provide 2025 totals but no monthly trend. This is a meaningful reduction in labor market visibility for Massachusetts.
