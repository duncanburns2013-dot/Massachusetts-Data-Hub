Labor Market Overview
Massachusetts added just 1,300 jobs in all of 2025 — the weakest year since the pandemic

⚠️ Hiring Recession

Only 1,300 jobs added in 2025 — while 27,000 more people became unemployed. BLS CES & LAUS — employers in "low-hire, low-fire" mode.

⚠️ Only State to Extend Benefits

MA extended unemployment benefits to 30 weeks — the only state to do so. Standard duration is 26 weeks.
